Many students and young people struggle to organise their daily responsibilities, homework, and household chores effectively. Contemporary productivity and task management applications are not as user-friendly, cluttered with unnecessary features, or not designed for simple routine scheduling. As a result, users may forget important tasks, lose track of weekly responsibilities, or struggle to maintain consistent routines.

The application will address this problem for students aged 15 and above who require simple and intuitive web applications for organising chores and assign time to complete these tasks while being simple and user-friendly.
